    7d & \hspace*{1.5em}\texttt{minimum\_spanning\_}\newline\hspace*{1.5em}\texttt{tree()} & \texttt{routes.py} & Creates the roads: connects the stops with the shortest total road length \\
    8 & \texttt{load\_or\_gen\_profiles()} & \texttt{loader.py} & Loads the destination profiles, or builds them from the categories dict in \texttt{datagen.py} \\
    9 & \texttt{save\_profiles()} & \texttt{loader.py} & Saves the destination profiles to \texttt{destination\_profile.csv} \\
